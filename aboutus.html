Who We Are

Shop Finder is a Rwanda-based platform designed to bridge the gap between local shop owners and everyday customers. We believe that finding a trusted shop should never be difficult.

Our platform allows verified shop owners to register and list their products, while customers can easily search, compare prices, and discover the best deals in their area — all in one place.

Built with simplicity and trust at its core, Shop Finder is your go-to directory for smarter, safer local shopping.

Connecting Shops & Shoppers

From registering your shop to finding the best price near you — Shop Finder makes every step simple and seamless.

Our Mission & Vision

What drives us every day and where we aim to go.

Our Mission

To empower local shop owners by giving them a digital presence, while helping customers make informed, confident shopping decisions. We simplify the search for trusted shops and fair prices across Rwanda.

Our Vision

To become the leading local shop discovery platform in East Africa — a trusted space where every shop owner can grow their business and every customer can shop smarter, faster, and safer.
